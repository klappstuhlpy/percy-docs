---
title: "My economy numbers look different"
description: "Why your payouts differ from the guide or from another server."
---

The numbers in this guide are the defaults. Three things change them.

## 1. The server's payout multiplier

An admin can scale every earning command from 0.1 to 10.

- Run `?economy-config` to see the current multiplier. You need the **Administrator** permission.
- Percy adds `(x1.50 payout bonus)` to a result when a bonus applies.

## 2. Your prestige bonus

Each prestige level adds a permanent +10% to your payouts, up to +100%. The multiplier and your prestige bonus are multiplied together. See [Prestige](/earning/overview#prestige).

## 3. Tools and boosts

- Without the matching tool, `?fish`, `?hunt` and `?dig` pay **60%** and find no items. See [Tools](/earning/activities#tools).
- A **loot boost** item raises `?fish`, `?hunt` and `?dig` for a while. `/perks` shows active boosts.

## Other reasons

| Symptom | Reason |
|---|---|
| `/daily` pays more or less than 250 | The admin changed the daily base, or your streak is higher |
| Items sell for half the price | That is the fixed sell rate: 50% |
| Net worth is higher than cash plus bank | `/balance` adds what your items sell for |
| A game rejects your bet | The admin set a maximum bet |
| Your balance is 0 on a new server | Balances are separate per server |
| Your coins vanished after prestige | Prestige resets cash and bank, never items |
