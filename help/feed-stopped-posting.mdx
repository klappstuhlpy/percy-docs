---
title: "A log or feed channel stopped posting"
description: "Fix audit logs, alerts, polls and feeds that went quiet."
---

If Percy can't post in a channel, it usually can't tell you there. It raises a **missing-permission alert** instead.

## Where Percy tells you

- In your **alerts channel**, if you set one. If you didn't, Percy tries the server's system channel.
- In the **Feed** on the dashboard's **Notifications** page. See [Change log and notifications](/dashboard/changes-notifications).

The alert names the action Percy couldn't do and the channel, for example "I don't have permission to announce giveaway winners".

## Fix it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Setup status">
    The dashboard's [Setup status](/dashboard/setup-status) names every channel Percy can't post in and the exact missing permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the channel permissions">
    Give Percy **View Channel**, **Send Messages** and **Embed Links** in that channel. A deny on the channel beats the role. For log channels, add **Manage Webhooks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-pick the channel if it still fails">
    The audit log posts through a webhook. If the webhook was deleted, choose the channel again so Percy recreates it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">
    On the dashboard, click **Send test** next to the log channel. Percy posts one harmless message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other causes

- The channel was deleted. Pick a new one.
- The log type is switched off. See [Set up logging](/dashboard/logging).
- Percy lost a server permission such as **Ban Members** or **Manage Roles**. Percy reports the loss in your alerts.

<Tip>
  Set an alerts channel once. Then you hear about every permission problem as it happens.
</Tip>
