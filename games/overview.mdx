---
title: "Games"
description: "Casino and party games that use the server economy."
---

Casino games bet cash from your wallet. Winnings pay out in cash and follow the server's payout multiplier. Admins can cap bets with `?economy-config max-bet`. `/games` lists every game.

| Game | Command | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Blackjack | `/blackjack <bet>` | Hit, stand, double, split, insurance or surrender against the dealer |
| Coin flip | `/coinflip <bet> [side]` | Against Percy, or duel a member for the pot |
| Poker | `/poker <stack>` | Texas Hold'em, up to 4 players; heads-up all-ins can run it twice |
| Roulette | `/roulette <bet> <space>` | Number, colour or range; bets close after 60 seconds |
| Slots | `/slots <bet>` | Spin for a multiplier |
| Tower | `/tower [bet]` | Climb floors, cash out before falling |
| Horse race | `/horserace <bet> <horse>` | Six horses, parimutuel payout |
| Mines | `/mines <bet> [mines]` | Reveal gems, avoid mines, cash out any time |
| Higher or lower | `/higherlower <bet>` | Guess the next card for a rising multiplier |
| Dice | `/dice <bet> <target>` | Bet on the total of two dice; rarer totals pay more |
| Russian roulette | `/russianroulette [ante]` | Shared pot, last player standing wins |
| Crash | `/crash <bet> [auto]` | Cash out before the multiplier crashes; optional auto cash-out |
| Plinko | `?plinko <bet> [rows] [risk]` | Drop a ball through 8, 12 or 16 rows; higher risk widens the payouts |
| Keno | `?keno <bet> <numbers>` | Pick 1–10 numbers from 40 (or a count for a quick pick); ten are drawn |
| Scratch card | `/scratch <bet>` | Scratch three of nine tiles; win their average multiplier |
| Snake eyes | `/snakeeyes <bet>` | One 1 pays x2, double 1s pay x15 |
| Fight | `/fight <member> [bet]` | Turn-based duel, optionally for coins |
| Heist | `/heist <member>` | Rally a crew to rob a member's bank |
| Minesweeper | `/minesweeper [mines]` | No coins at stake |
| Wordle | `/wordle` | Daily 5-letter word, 6 tries; correct answers pay coins |
| Trivia | `/trivia` | First correct answer wins the reward |
| Tic-tac-toe | `/tictactoe <member>` | Two players |
| Hangman | `/hangman` | Guess the word letter by letter |

## Lottery

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lottery`, `/lottery status` | Jackpot, ticket price, tickets sold, your tickets and odds, time left |
| `/lottery buy [amount]` | Buy tickets with cash |
| `/lottery start <duration> <ticket_price>` | Admin: start a lottery (duration 5 minutes to 30 days) |

The jackpot is drawn when the timer ends and paid to one ticket holder.
