---
title: "Pets"
description: "Adopt a pet that earns coins while you're away, and keep it fed."
---

A pet earns coins for you, even when you are offline. You can have one pet per server.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/pet` | Your pet: hunger, unclaimed earnings and feeding cost |
| `/pet shop` | The species you can adopt, with prices |
| `/pet adopt <species> [name]` | Adopt a pet |
| `/pet feed` | Feed your pet so it earns at full rate |
| `/pet claim` | Collect what it earned |
| `/pet rename <name>` | Give it a new name |
| `/pet abandon` | Release it. You get no refund |

## Species

| Species | Cost | Earns | Stores up to | Feeding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hamster | 500 | 8 per hour | 12 hours | 25 |
| Cat | 2,500 | 20 per hour | 18 hours | 60 |
| Dog | 3,000 | 26 per hour | 14 hours | 80 |
| Parrot | 6,000 | 45 per hour | 20 hours | 120 |
| Fox | 12,000 | 80 per hour | 24 hours | 200 |
| Dragon | 50,000 | 250 per hour | 36 hours | 600 |

Earnings stop adding up once your pet has stored its maximum hours. Claim before that.

<Warning>
  Hunger lowers earnings. A pet that wasn't fed for 18 hours earns at half rate. After 36 hours it earns nothing.
</Warning>

You can also feed and collect from the **Pet** page of `/economy`.
