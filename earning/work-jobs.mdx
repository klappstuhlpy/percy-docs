---
title: "Work and jobs"
description: "Work shifts, unlock better jobs and climb the career ladder."
---

`?work` pays once an hour. Every shift counts toward unlocking a better job.

## Work a shift

```text
?work
```

Each shift rolls a payout in your job's range. About 3 shifts in 10 roll an event:

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Overtime | 1.5x pay |
| Tip | 1.25x pay |
| Mishap | Half pay |

## The career ladder

Everyone starts as a **Freelancer**. Your **lifetime shifts** unlock the next jobs.

| Job | Pay per shift | Lifetime shifts to unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer | 20 to 250 | Everyone starts here |
| Barista | 80 to 300 | 5 |
| Cashier | 140 to 420 | 15 |
| Line Cook | 220 to 560 | 30 |
| Electrician | 320 to 750 | 55 |
| Programmer | 450 to 1,000 | 90 |
| Surgeon | 620 to 1,350 | 140 |
| Astronaut | 850 to 1,800 | 200 |

Unlocking a job doesn't switch you to it. Apply for it:

```text
/job list
/job apply Barista
```

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/job` | Your job, lifetime shifts and progress to the next unlock |
| `/job list` | The whole ladder |
| `/job apply <name>` | Switch to a job you unlocked |
| `/job quit` | Go back to Freelancer. Lifetime shifts are kept |

<Note>
  Lifetime shifts never reset. Switching jobs, quitting or [prestiging](/earning/overview#prestige) all keep them.
</Note>

Pay is scaled by the server's payout multiplier and your prestige bonus.
