Every member has a wallet on each server. Your coins on one server are separate from your coins on any other.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/home-4-economy-hub.webp" alt="The economy hub card showing balance, job, pet and quests" />
</Frame>

Run `/economy` to see your balance, job, pet, quests, badges and perks on one card. Use the menu to switch pages.

## Cash and bank

| Where | Use it for | Can be robbed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash** | Spending: the shop, games, transfers | Yes |
| **Bank** | Safe storage | No |

Move coins between them for free:

```text
/deposit 500
/withdraw 500
```

Games and the shop always use **cash**. Withdraw first if your coins are in the bank.

## The commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/balance [@member]` | Cash, bank, the value of your items, net worth and server rank |
| `/balance leaderboard` | The richest members |
| `/transfer @member <amount>` | Send cash to another member |
| `/daily`, `?weekly`, `?monthly` | Timed rewards. See [Daily, weekly and monthly](/earning/daily-rewards) |
| `?work` | A shift at your job. See [Work and jobs](/earning/work-jobs) |
| `?fish`, `?hunt`, `?dig` | Pay more with a tool. See [Fishing, hunting and digging](/earning/activities) |
| `?beg`, `?search` | Quick, low-stakes cash |
| `/pet` | A pet that earns for you. See [Pets](/earning/pets) |
| `/quests`, `/achievements` | Daily goals and badges. See [Quests and achievements](/earning/quests-achievements) |
| `/rob @member` | Steal cash from someone. See [Rob and shields](/earning/rob-shields) |

<Note>
  **Net worth** in `/balance` is cash plus bank plus what your items sell for. [Prestige](#prestige) checks only cash plus bank.
</Note>

## Payout multiplier

Every earning command is scaled by two numbers multiplied together:

- The **payout multiplier** your server sets, from 0.1 to 10. The default is 1.
- Your **prestige bonus**.

When a bonus applies, Percy adds a note such as `(x1.20 payout bonus)` to the result. If your numbers differ from a friend's on another server, read [My economy numbers look different](/help/economy-numbers).

## Prestige

When your cash plus bank reaches the requirement, you can prestige. Percy resets your **cash and bank to 0** and gives you a permanent **+10% payout bonus** for each prestige level.

```text
?prestige
```

Run it without the requirement to see your progress. Percy asks you to confirm before it resets anything.

| Prestige level | Cash + bank needed | Bonus afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 250,000 | +10% |
| 2 | 500,000 | +20% |
| 3 | 750,000 | +30% |
| each level after | +250,000 more | +10% more |
| 10 | 2,500,000 | +100% (the maximum) |

<Note>
  Prestige keeps your items, job, lifetime shifts, pet, quests and achievements. Only cash and bank reset.
</Note>
