## Rob a member

```text
/rob @member
```

You can steal part of another member's **cash**. Their bank is safe. You can rob once a day.

- Your chance of failing is your cash plus bank, divided by (their cash plus your cash plus bank). Percy keeps it between 20% and 80%. The richer you are compared to your target, the riskier it gets.
- If you succeed, you take your success chance times their cash.
- If you fail, you lose coins as a fine.

Percy doesn't let you rob a bot, yourself, or someone with no cash.

<Tip>
  Keep your coins in the bank. A robber can only take cash.
</Tip>

## Protect yourself

A **rob shield** is a shop item. When you use it, nobody can rob you until it expires. A blocked attempt still costs the robber their cooldown.

```text
/buy Rob Shield
/use Rob Shield
```

The item name depends on your server's shop. See [Using items](/items/using-items) for how timed effects work and `/perks` to see what is active.

## For admins

Turn robbing off for the whole server:

```text
?economy-config rob off
```

See [Server economy settings](/earning/server-settings).
