---

Three commands pay a fixed reward on a timer.

| Command | Default reward | Wait between claims |
|---|---|---|
| `/daily` | 250, growing with your streak | 24 hours |
| `?weekly` | 1,500 | 7 days |
| `?monthly` | 7,000 | 30 days |

Claim too early and Percy tells you when the reward is ready.

## Daily streak

Claim `/daily` again within 48 hours of your last claim and your streak grows. Each streak day adds 50 coins to the base reward.

| Streak day | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | 250 |
| 2 | 300 |
| 3 | 350 |
| 4 | 400 |
| 5 | 450 |
| 6 | 500 |
| 7 | 550 |
| 8 and later | 600 (the maximum) |

If more than 48 hours pass, your streak starts again at day 1.

<Tip>
  Claim at about the same time each day. You can claim every 24 hours, so a small drift never breaks the streak.
</Tip>

## What changes the numbers

- An admin can change the base daily reward. See [Server economy settings](/earning/server-settings).
- Every reward is scaled by the server's payout multiplier and your prestige bonus. See [Earning money](/earning/overview#payout-multiplier).
- A 7-day and a 30-day streak each unlock an [achievement](/earning/quests-achievements).
