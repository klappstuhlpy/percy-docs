## Daily quests

Every day you get **three quests**. Progress counts while you play, and the reward pays out the moment a quest completes.

```text
/quests
```

| Quest | What counts |
|---|---|
| Catch fish | `?fish` |
| Bag hunts | `?hunt` |
| Work shifts | `?work` |
| Beg | `?beg` |
| Dig up finds | `?dig` |
| Search locations | `?search` |
| Deposit cash | `/deposit` |
| Gift items | `/gift` |

Goals and rewards change from day to day. The board resets at midnight UTC and is the same for everyone on the server.

## Achievements

Achievements are badges. Each one pays a one-time cash reward when you unlock it.

```text
/achievements
/achievements @member
```

| Badge | How to unlock | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Pocket Money | Net worth of 1,000 | 100 |
| Entrepreneur | Net worth of 10,000 | 250 |
| Six Figures | Net worth of 100,000 | 1,000 |
| Millionaire | Net worth of 1,000,000 | 5,000 |
| Dedicated | 7-day daily streak | 500 |
| Unstoppable | 30-day daily streak | 2,500 |
| First Shift | Work your first shift | 100 |
| Workaholic | Work 100 shifts | 1,000 |
| Top of the Ladder | Get hired as Astronaut | 2,000 |
| Ascended | Prestige for the first time | 2,500 |
| Pet Parent | Adopt a pet | 200 |
| Quartermaster | Complete 25 daily quests | 1,000 |
| Collector | Own 50 items at once | 500 |
