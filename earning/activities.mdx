These commands pay from a loot table. Each has its own cooldown.

| Command | Cooldown | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| `?fish` | 60 seconds | Catches from an old boot to a rare pearl |
| `?hunt` | 90 seconds | Bigger and riskier payouts |
| `?dig` | 90 seconds | Finds from bottle caps to ancient relics |
| `?beg` | 45 seconds | Usually a little, sometimes nothing, rarely a windfall |
| `?search` | 75 seconds | Pick one of three spots. Risky spots pay more but can cost cash |

## Tools

`?fish`, `?hunt` and `?dig` work best with the matching tool. Buy it in the shop.

| Tool | Price | Uses | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fishing Rod | 600 | 25 | `?fish` |
| Hunting Rifle | 1,500 | 20 | `?hunt` |
| Shovel | 500 | 25 | `?dig` |

| | Without the tool | With the tool |
|---|---|---|
| Cash payout | 60% | 100% |
| Item drops | None | 35% chance on each use |

Each use of the command spends one use of the tool. When the last use is spent, that tool is gone and the next one you own starts fresh. The item card shows how many uses are left.

```text
/buy Fishing Rod
?fish
```

<Tip>
  Without a tool, Percy adds a reminder to buy one. Own several tools of the same kind to keep going without a gap.
</Tip>

## Item drops

With the right tool, a command can also drop an item. Drops are added to your inventory and have a rarity. You can [sell, gift or trade](/items/selling-gifting) them.

| Command | Possible drops |
|---|---|
| `?fish` | Sardine, Salmon, Pufferfish, Golden Fish |
| `?hunt` | Rabbit Hide, Boar Tusk, Bear Pelt, Stag Antlers |
| `?dig` | Bent Spoon, Ancient Coin, Jade Idol, Ancient Relic |

Rarer drops are much less likely. Drops can't be bought in the shop.

## Search

`?search` shows three random spots with buttons. Safe spots like your couch never go wrong. Riskier spots pay more but can injure you and cost cash. You never lose more cash than you have.

## What changes your payout

- A **loot boost** item makes `?fish`, `?hunt` and `?dig` pay more for a while. See [Using items](/items/using-items).
- The server's payout multiplier and your prestige bonus scale everything. See [Earning money](/earning/overview#payout-multiplier).
- Most of these commands also advance your [daily quests](/earning/quests-achievements).
