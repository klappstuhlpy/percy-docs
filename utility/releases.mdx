## Subscriptions

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/subscribe <subject> [streams]` | Follow a subject; pick it from the suggestions |
| `/subscriptions` | List, change or remove what you follow |
| `/unsubscribe <subject>` | Stop following |

| Comics | Film and TV |
|---|---|
| Publisher (Marvel, DC, Manga), series, creator, character | Universe, franchise, person, character |

`streams` is `releases` (default), `news` or `both`. Percy sends an hourly DM digest. If your DMs are closed, Percy mutes your subscriptions instead of retrying; re-enable them from the dashboard.

## Public pages

Server-rendered and readable without logging in. Signed in, matching items for what you follow are highlighted.

| Page | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`/releases`](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/releases) | What landed and what is imminent across both media |
| [`/news`](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/news) | Headlines tagged by subject, with source and link |
| [`/comics`](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/comics) | Comic catalogue: releases, series, credits |
| [`/watchlist`](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/watchlist) | Film and TV watch orders, with a trailer link per title |

Signed in, each card has a read/watched toggle. Manage subscriptions at `/account/subscriptions`. News shows the headline, a short excerpt, the source and a link to the original, never the article body.
