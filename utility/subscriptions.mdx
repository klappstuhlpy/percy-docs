A subscription is a standing interest. You tell Percy what you care about — a comic series, the artist drawing it, a film universe, the actor you follow across everything — and Percy sends you a direct message when something you follow lands. Subscriptions belong to you, not to a server: they follow you everywhere Percy is, and no one else sees what you follow.

Subscriptions are entirely opt-in. Percy never DMs you until you follow your first thing.

## Commands

| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `/subscribe <subject> [streams]` | Follow something. Pick the subject from the suggestions. |
| `/subscriptions` | See everything you follow, and change or remove any of it. |
| `/unsubscribe <subject>` | Stop following one thing. |

All three reply privately, so running them in a busy channel does not tell everyone what you read.

## What you can follow

Percy suggests subjects from its own catalogue as you type, across two media:

| Comics | Film & TV |
|---|---|
| A **publisher** — Marvel, DC or Manga (every release) | A **universe** — a whole watch order |
| A **series** | A **franchise** within a universe |
| A **creator** — writer, artist, anyone credited | A **person** — actor, director or writer |
| A **character** | A **character** |

<Warning>
  You have to pick one of the suggestions. Percy matches new releases against the exact key behind each suggestion, so a name typed freehand would create a subscription that never matches anything — Percy rejects it rather than let that happen silently.
</Warning>

Following a person works the same way on both sides: one subscription covers everything they are credited on, whether they acted in it, directed it or wrote it.

## Choosing which updates you get

Every subscription carries a **streams** setting:

| Value | You get |
|---|---|
| `releases` | New releases only. This is the default. |
| `news` | News and articles only. |
| `both` | Both. |

Releases is the default because news is far more frequent: a busy franchise can produce a dozen stories in the time it produces one release. Switch a row to `both` when you want the conversation as well as the shipping date.

<Note>
  News is delivered in the same digest as releases, never as extra messages, and the two are capped separately — a busy news week can never crowd out the release you actually subscribed for.
</Note>

## What the DM looks like

Percy checks for new items every hour and sends you **one digest per check**, never one message per item:

- Items are grouped by medium and release date, with anything announced but undated listed first.
- Up to a dozen releases are shown; anything beyond that is summarised as "…and N more".
- Up to five news stories ride along in the same message, each with the name of the outlet that published it and a link to the original. Percy never reproduces the article — you get the headline and a teaser, and you read the rest at the source.
- Anything that does not fit either cap is not lost: it leads your next digest.
- A comic appears when Percy first learns about it; a film or show appears on the day it is released. Film and TV dates are re-checked against TMDB nightly, so a date that moves moves your digest with it.
- You are told about an item **once**, no matter how many of your subscriptions match it.
- Following something today never dumps its backlog — only items Percy learned about after you subscribed reach you.

## Managing what you follow

Run `/subscriptions` for a list grouped by medium, with two controls underneath:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Remove a subscription">
    Pick a row from the first menu to unfollow it immediately. `/unsubscribe` does the same thing in one step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change which updates a row sends">
    Pick a row from the second menu to cycle its streams: releases → both → news → releases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The same list lives on the web at [percy.klappstuhl.me/account/subscriptions](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/account/subscriptions), where you can also search the whole catalogue for something to follow and mute a subscription without removing it.

Everything you can follow is also browsable without signing in — see [Releases & news](/utility/catalogue), which is also where you mark what you have already read or watched. Following and marking are separate: a subscription decides what Percy tells you about, a mark records what you got to.

## If your DMs are closed

Percy cannot tell when a closed DM opens again, so it never retries forever. The first time a digest cannot be delivered, every one of your subscriptions is **muted** — nothing is deleted, and the list is exactly as you left it.

To start receiving digests again, open your DMs for the server you share with Percy, then either un-mute the subscription on the [website](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/account/subscriptions) or run `/subscribe` on it again.

<Tip>
  Following a whole publisher is a firehose. If Marvel's full weekly list is too much, follow the two or three series you actually read instead — or follow the creator, which follows them onto whatever they work on next.
</Tip>
