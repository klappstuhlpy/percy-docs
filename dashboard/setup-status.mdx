---
title: "Check your setup status"
description: "See which features Percy can really use, and fix the ones that are blocked."
---

A setting can be saved and still not work. Percy may not be allowed to post in the channel, or its role may sit below a role it should give out. **Setup status** checks this live against Discord.

## Where to find it

Open your server's **Configuration** page. **Setup status** is at the top. It opens by itself when something needs attention.

## What it checks

For every feature you use, Percy checks the channels and roles you picked:

| Feature | Checked |
|---|---|
| Audit log, Moderation log, Message log, Voice log, Alerts | Percy can see and post in the channel |
| Polls | Percy can post in the poll channel |
| Mute role | The role exists and sits below Percy's role |
| Leveling | The level-up channel and every level reward role |
| Music panel | The panel channel |
| Sentinel verification | The verification channel and the verified role |

Each problem names the channel or role and the exact permission that is missing.

## Fix a problem

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the problem">
    A line such as "Percy can't post in #logs" tells you what is wrong and where.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Fix">
    **Fix** opens the page for that feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it in Discord">
    Give Percy the permission on the channel, or move Percy's role above the role. See [Permissions Percy needs](/getting-started/permissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload">
    The check runs again every time you load the page. It is never cached.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Features you don't use appear under **Not in use**, with a **Set up** button.

<Tip>
  Channel and role pickers across the dashboard also mark channels Percy can't post in and roles it can't give out, before you save.
</Tip>
