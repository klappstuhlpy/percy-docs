---

Both live on the **Notifications** page of the dashboard. It has four tabs: **Feed**, **Requests**, **Change log** and **Settings**.

## Notifications feed

The **Feed** tab lists things worth knowing:

- Percy can't do something because a permission is missing.
- A webhook was disabled.
- The outcome of a moderation [action request](/moderation/requests).
- Notices from the Percy team, marked **global**.

Unread entries have a dot, and the sidebar shows a badge. Click **Mark all read** to clear it. Entries are removed after 90 days.

Below the feed, **Recent moderation** lists the last 7 days of cases.

## Change log

The **Change log** tab shows who changed what on the dashboard, and when.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| When | Time of the change |
| Who | The person, or `api:<token name>` for a change made with an [API token](/dashboard/developers) |
| What | The action and the page |
| Fields | The names of the settings that changed |

<Note>
  Percy keeps the **names** of changed settings, never their values. Entries are kept for 90 days. Changes you make with commands in Discord are not listed.
</Note>

## Action requests

The **Requests** and **Settings** tabs are where moderators decide requests and where you choose the request channel and requester roles. See [Action requests](/moderation/requests).
