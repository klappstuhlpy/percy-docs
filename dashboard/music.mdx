The dashboard's music panel is a small music app: a player bar, your server's queue, search, play history and your own library.

## Where to find it

Open **Music** from your account menu or the button on the `/dashboard` server picker, or go to `/dashboard/music` and pick a server from the picker at the top. A server's public overview has an **Open the music app** link, and the server dashboard sidebar's **Music** entry (Manage Server) opens the same app with a **Back to server dashboard** link.

## What you can do

- **Listen along:** see what is playing, the queue, time-synced lyrics and who is listening.
- **Search and play:** search for a track and start it in the server's player. You need to be in the player's voice channel.
- **History:** the **Recently played** list shows what played in the server. It fills as tracks play.
- **Your library:** your liked songs and playlists, the same ones the `playlist` commands use.
- **Queue controls:** remove or reorder queued tracks. This needs the DJ role or the Manage Server permission, in every DJ mode.
- **Manager tools:** setup, reset, equalizer, filters, 24/7 and DJ mode need Manage Server. Items that need the DJ role carry a DJ chip and admin-only ones an Admin chip in the side navigation. With JS on, **Session settings** shows the DJ mode and 24/7 sections together, and changes to DJ mode are saved with the page's unsaved-changes banner.

## Empty states

An empty queue shows "The queue is empty", and a server without history shows "Nothing has played here yet".

## Small screens

Below 1024 px the right-hand now-playing panel moves under the main content. Below 768 px the side navigation shrinks to icons, and the player bar stays at the bottom.
