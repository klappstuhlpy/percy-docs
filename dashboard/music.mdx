The dashboard's music panel is a small music app: a player bar, your server's queue, search, play history and your own library.

## Where to find it

Open **Music** from your account menu or the button on the `/dashboard` server picker, or go to `/dashboard/music` and pick a server from the picker at the top. A server's public overview has an **Open the music app** link, and the server dashboard sidebar's **Music** entry (Manage Server) opens the same app with a **Back to server dashboard** link.

## What you can do

- **Listen along:** see what is playing, the queue, time-synced lyrics and who is listening.
- **Search and play:** search for a track and start it in the server's player. You need to be in the player's voice channel.
- **History:** the **Recently played** list shows what played in the server. It fills as tracks play.
- **Your library:** your liked songs and playlists, the same ones the `playlist` commands use. Liked songs and playlist songs show their artist and cover.
- **Playlists:** create, rename and delete playlists. Hover the cover on a playlist's page and click **Change cover** to upload an image, use an image link, or go back to the first song's cover. You can also drop an image file onto the cover.
- **Add to playlist:** every song's **•••** menu has **Add to playlist**, and the player bar has an **Add to playlist** button next to the heart. On larger screens you can also drag any song (from the queue, search, history or a list) onto **Liked Songs** or a playlist in the side navigation.
- **Trending:** **Trending this week** on the home view shows this server's and Percy's most-picked songs of the last 7 days.
- **Server picker:** a green dot and **Playing now** mark every server where Percy is playing.
- **Queue controls:** remove or reorder queued tracks. This needs the DJ role or the Manage Server permission, in every DJ mode.
- **Manager tools:** setup, reset, equalizer, filters, 24/7 and DJ mode need Manage Server. 24/7 autoplay can start from the song that is playing, so you can leave its source empty. Items that need the DJ role carry a DJ chip and admin-only ones an Admin chip in the side navigation. With JS on, **Session settings** shows the DJ mode and 24/7 sections together, and changes to DJ mode are saved with the page's unsaved-changes banner.

## Empty states

An empty queue shows "The queue is empty", and a server without history shows "Nothing has played here yet".

## Small screens

Below 1024 px the right-hand now-playing panel moves under the main content. Below 768 px the side navigation shrinks to icons, and the player bar stays at the bottom.
