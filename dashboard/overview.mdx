The [dashboard](https://percy.klappstuhl.me/dashboard/) lets you manage Percy without typing commands. Everything you save there is applied by Percy right away.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/home-11-dashboard-overview.webp" alt="The server overview page of the Percy dashboard" />
</Frame>

## Log in and open a server

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in with Discord">
    Click **Dashboard** and approve the Discord prompt. Percy asks to see who you are and which servers you are in. It can't read your messages or act as you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a server">
    Your servers are listed in three groups:

    - **Managed**: servers where Percy is present and you can manage it.
    - **Servers you're in**: servers with Percy where you can't manage it. You can still open your own pages there.
    - **Add Percy**: servers you manage that don't have Percy yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the sidebar">
    The sidebar groups the pages: **Overview**, **Features**, **Community** and **Integrations**. Press `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K`) to search for a page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can manage a server

You can manage a server in the dashboard if you own it or have the **Manage Server** permission. If a server you manage is missing or shows as not manageable, read [The dashboard says I can't manage my server](/help/dashboard-cant-manage).

## Pages at a glance

| Sidebar group | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Notifications, Configuration, Members, AutoMod, Statistics, Analytics, Commands |
| **Features** | Leveling, Economy, Music, Autoresponders, Comics, Temp Channels |
| **Community** | Polls, Image Gallery, Giveaways, Tags, Role Menus, Starboard, Highlights, Emoji Stats |
| **Integrations** | Webhooks, Backup & Templates, Developers |
| **Personal** | My Profile |

<Note>
  Settings cards have no Save button. When you change something, a banner appears at the bottom. Save or discard all your changes at once.
</Note>
