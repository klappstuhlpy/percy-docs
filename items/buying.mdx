## Buy an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Have the cash">
    Purchases use **cash**. Withdraw from your bank first if you need to:

    ```text
    /withdraw 1000
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy it">
    Use the command, or press **Buy 1** or **Buy...** on the item in `/shop`:

    ```text
    /buy Lucky Charm 2
    ```

    The total is the price times the quantity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Autocomplete suggests item names as you type.
</Tip>

## Daily deals

Each day, up to **two** items in your server's shop are **10% to 40% off**. The deals change at midnight UTC and are the same for everyone on the server. They show at the top of `/shop`, and Percy adds the discount to your receipt, for example `(-25% today)`.

## Why a purchase can fail

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| You need X cash but only have Y | Withdraw more or buy fewer |
| Sold out, or only N left | The item has limited stock |
| You can hold at most N | The item has a per-member limit. It counts what you already own |
| Reserved for members with a role | You need the required role |
| No longer on sale | The item's sale period ended |
| Not sold in the shop | It is a drop. You can only find it |

## Limits an admin can set

- **Stock**: how many can be sold in total.
- **Per-member limit**: the most one person may hold.
- **Required role**: only members with that role can buy it.
- **End date**: the item stops being sold after a set time.

Two people buying the last copy at once can't oversell it. One of them gets the "sold out" message.
