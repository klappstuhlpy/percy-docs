---
title: "Trading"
description: "Swap items and cash with another member. Both of you confirm, and it happens all at once."
---

A trade is an exchange between two members. Either everything moves or nothing does.

```text
/trade @member
```

## How a trade works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the trade">
    Run `/trade @member`. Percy posts a card that shows both offers. You can't trade with yourself or with a bot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to your offer">
    Press **Add item** to pick one of your items and a quantity. Press **Add cash** to offer cash. Each side can have up to 10 lines (items plus cash).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take things back">
    Press **Remove...** to pull lines out of your own offer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Both confirm">
    When you are happy, press **Confirm**. The trade runs when **both** of you have confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Any change to either offer clears both confirmations. Check the card again before you confirm.
</Warning>

Press **Cancel** to call it off. The card closes by itself after 5 minutes.

## When a trade fails

Percy checks everything again at the moment of the swap. It fails, and nothing moves, if:

- Someone no longer has the cash they offered.
- Someone no longer owns the items they offered.
- An item would go over the receiver's per-member limit.

<Tip>
  Trading is the safe way to sell to a friend. A swap can never half-complete.
</Tip>
