---
title: "Selling and gifting items"
description: "Sell items back for half their price, or give them to another member."
---

## Sell an item

```text
/sell Lucky Charm 2
```

Percy pays **50% of the item's price** for each copy, rounded down. In your inventory, press **Sell 1** or **Sell all** on the item's page.

This works for every item you own, including the fish, hides and relics you find.

<Note>
  Selling price follows the item's current shop price. Your balance card shows **Items**: what your whole inventory would sell for. It counts toward your net worth.
</Note>

## Gift an item

```text
/gift @member Lucky Charm 3
```

Gifting moves items to another member. No coins change hands. In your inventory, press **Gift...**, then pick the member and the quantity.

Percy refuses a gift when:

- The receiver is a bot, or yourself.
- You don't own that many.
- The receiver would go over the item's per-member limit.

Gifting counts toward the daily **gift** [quest](/earning/quests-achievements).

## Swap instead

To exchange items for items or cash, use [Trading](/items/trading).
