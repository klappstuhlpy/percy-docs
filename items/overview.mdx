Items are things you buy, find, use, sell, gift or trade. The **shop** sells them and your **inventory** holds them. Both are the same interactive card.

## The commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/shop` | Open the shop |
| `/inventory [@member]` | Open your inventory, or read someone else's |
| `/item <name>` | One item's price, effect, stock and what you can do with it |
| `/buy <item> [quantity]` | Buy from the shop. See [Buying items](/items/buying) |
| `/use <item> [quantity]` | Use an item. See [Using items](/items/using-items) |
| `/sell <item> [quantity]` | Sell back for half the price |
| `/gift @member <item> [quantity]` | Give items away. See [Selling and gifting](/items/selling-gifting) |
| `/trade @member` | Swap items and cash. See [Trading](/items/trading) |
| `/perks [@member]` | Your active boosts |

## The card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse">
    The list shows five items per page with a **View** button on each. Use the category menu to filter. Items are sorted by rarity, then name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    The detail page shows the price, what you'd get from selling it, how many you own, what it does, and any stock, limit or required role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act">
    In the shop, press **Buy 1** or **Buy...**. In your inventory, press **Use**, **Sell 1**, **Sell all** or **Gift...**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Someone else's inventory is read-only: you see their items but get no buttons.

## Categories

The category comes from what the item does. You can't set it by hand.

| Category | Items with this effect |
|---|---|
| Consumables | `cash`, `lootbox` |
| Boosts | `xp_boost`, `loot_boost`, `rob_shield` |
| Role items | `role` |
| Collectibles | No effect |

## Rarity

Items are **Common**, **Uncommon**, **Rare**, **Epic** or **Legendary**. Rarity is cosmetic. It changes sorting and the card colour. It never changes an item's price or effect.

## Percy's items and your server's items

| | Percy built-in items | Server items |
|---|---|---|
| Who makes them | Percy | Your admins |
| Examples | Fishing Rod, Hunting Rifle, Shovel, and the fish, hides and relics you find | Anything the admins add |
| Editable | No. Name, price and drops are fixed | Yes |
| In the shop | Tools only. Drops can't be bought | Yes |

Built-in items exist on every server and can't be removed, renamed or reused as a name for your own item. Tools are explained in [Fishing, hunting and digging](/earning/activities#tools).
